Why AloeFlex

Every feature. Every athlete.

We engineered a brace that doesn't make you choose between comfort and protection.

Aloe Vera Microencapsulation

Capsules release aloe vera during wear — soothing inflammation, fighting bacteria, and naturally regulating temperature.

LDPE Impact Shell

Low Density Polyethylene with 7,830 PSI tensile strength distributes impact while staying flexible for full movement.

Premium neoprene and memory foam conform to your joint shape. Elastic cotton outer ensures breathability and stretch.
