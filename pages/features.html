Aloe Vera Microencapsulation

The interior fabric of all AloeFlex products is infused with aloe vera using microencapsulation technology. When the fabric is worn, the capsules release aloe vera onto the skin, providing continuous soothing benefits throughout use.

Impact Protection

LDPE Impact Shell

AloeFlex balances flexibility and protection using Low Density Polyethylene (LDPE) — a plastic that is extremely durable whilst being simultaneously flexible. The LDPE shell has a maximum tensile strength of 7,830 PSI, far exceeding forces experienced in real sports settings.
