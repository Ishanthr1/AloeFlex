Technical Specifications

Shell Material

Low Density Polyethylene

LDPE provides 7,830 PSI tensile strength — far exceeding any force an athlete would experience in play. Flexible yet highly resistant to blunt impact.

Interior Fabric

Aloe Vera Microencapsulation

Aloe vera capsules release continuously during wear. Anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, anti-allergenic, and naturally temperature regulating.

Core Material

Neoprene + Memory Foam

Medical-grade neoprene and memory foam contour precisely to the knee joint. Elastic cotton outer layer adds breathability and four-way stretch.

Sensors

Contact Microphones

Embedded contact microphones detect micro-vibrations on the skin surface, enabling real-time joint health assessment and arthritis severity classification.

Sensors

EBI Sensors

Electrical Bioimpedance sensors quantitatively measure joint swelling and inflammation. Clinical-grade accuracy in a noninvasive wearable form factor.

Fit System

Dual Stability Straps

Dual adjustable straps provide loosening and tightening control across the full range of joint sizes. One brace that truly fits everyone.
