How It Works

Monitoring made simple

With the user's consent, the brace monitors the appendage it is on. AloeTrack accomplishes this using Contact Microphones and EBI Sensors. These data collection methods are all performed noninvasively. AloeTrack displays results and provides a general area of effect. If deemed necessary, AloeTrack will display an advisory warning against physical exertion and advise the user to contact a physician.

Additionally, AloeTrack monitors healing progression by analyzing past user data, giving patients an initial analysis if an injury occurs. Product owners can also contact customer support through the app. AloeTrack is the future of joint health monitoring and currently stands alone in the orthopedics and braces market.
