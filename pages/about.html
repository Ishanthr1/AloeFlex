The Problem

Why AloeFlex exists

The health of athletes in professional and casual sports is a significant concern. High intensity sports like basketball and football cause injuries such as ACL tears and wrist fractures. Over 3.5 million injuries occur annually — 40% of which are career-ending.

While existing joint braces aim to prevent injuries, they often hinder performance, cause discomfort, and lead to skin irritation. When prevention fails, the lengthy rehabilitation process sidelines players and harms team morale. This highlights the urgent need for improved preventative and recovery methods.

Customer Segments

Who we serve

Athletes make up 16% of our target market — consisting of high school (870), college (420), and professional (70) players. They represent the most profitable demographic through team deals and sponsorships, generating $153,000 in combined revenue.

The everyday consumer makes up 84% of projected sales. These are people purchasing braces for casual and recreational use — at rec centers, parks, or for post-surgery recovery. This segment generates over $300,000 in revenue.

Analysis

SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • Flexible and breathable materials ensure comfort
  • Aloe vera cooling soothes skin and inflammation
  • LDPE impact protection at 7,830 PSI
  • AloeTrack injury tracking app included free

Weaknesses

  • Semi-expensive startup cost
  • Small initial market for team deals
  • Low initial market share (0.013%)

Opportunities

  • Capture larger market share as brand grows
  • Reduce expenses through scale in the long run
  • Positively impact player health of signed teams

Threats

  • Strong preexisting competition (3M, Enovis, Modvel)
  • Heavily reliant on shipping channels
  • No physical store location outside of retailers
